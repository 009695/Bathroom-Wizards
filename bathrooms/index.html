Questions

Things people ask us

How long will I be without a bathroom?

A full refit is usually six to nine working days. A suite swap is often one or two. We will tell you the realistic number before we start, and we do not disappear mid-job to go and start someone else's.

Do you add VAT on top?

No. We are not VAT registered, so the price we quote is the price you pay. On a full bathroom that is a genuine saving of several hundred pounds against a VAT-registered firm.

Can I supply my own suite and tiles?

Absolutely, and plenty of people do. Our labour-only prices are for exactly that. We are happy to come with you to the merchant and tell you honestly what is worth the money.

What if you find a problem once the old bathroom is out?

Old houses hide things: rotten floorboards, lead pipe, no proper waterproofing. If we find something we stop, show you, explain the options and what each costs. Nothing gets added to your bill without you agreeing to it first.

Do you do the electrics too?

Bathroom electrics are notifiable work, so anything needing a certificate goes through our qualified electrician. You get the paperwork at the end.

Is your work guaranteed?

Yes. Our workmanship is guaranteed for twelve months, and the suite and fittings carry the manufacturer's own warranty on top of that.
