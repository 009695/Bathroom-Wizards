What we do

No job too small. That is the whole point.

Most trades will not get out of the van for an hour's work. We will. If it is on this list we can do it, and if it is not, just ask.

Doors & locks

Lock changes, sticking doors eased, new handles and hinges, draught-proofing and door closers.

Taps & small plumbing

Dripping taps, new toilet seats, running cisterns, waste traps and isolating valves.

Painting & decorating

Walls, ceilings and woodwork, inside and out. Clean edges and properly prepared surfaces.

Shelving & mounting

Floating shelves, TV brackets, mirrors, curtain poles and flat-pack furniture built properly.

Tiling & sealing

Wall and floor tiling, re-grouting, fresh silicone around baths and showers, and bathroom cladding.

Fencing & outside jobs

Fence panels and posts, gate repairs and adjustments, and general garden carpentry.
